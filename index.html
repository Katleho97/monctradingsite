01 / ABOUT MONC


Founded in 2007, Monc Trading and Development Services (CC) has grown into a trusted provider of professional building, construction, and maintenance solutions for residential, commercial, and corporate clients.

Our expertise spans general building repairs, home additions, renovations, paving, tree removal, steel or aluminium work, fencing solutions, precast walls, retaining walls, farm fencing, plumbing, plus the supply and delivery of building materials. We also specialize in ceiling installations, roofing projects, insurance assessments with detailed reporting, aluminium doors, windows, glass fittings, sliding motor gate automation, and garage doors. For corporate spaces, we deliver tailored construction solutions that emphasize quality workmanship, reliable service, and practical outcomes.

What sets Monc apart is our experienced team combined with modern maintenance methods. We focus on keeping every space safe, functional, dependable. Our approach is proactive — addressing issues when they arise while preventing future problems, ensuring the environments we care for continue to perform at their best for years to come.

We are proud to be accredited, fully compliant, and registered with NHBRC, COIDA and CIDB , reflecting our commitment to industry standards and client trust.
